## 🎬 Narrative Scenario & Dialogue Prompts

### ⚡ Scenario 1: "The Late-Night Subnet Breach"
* **Context:** 02:40 AM. An automated intrusion attempt hits the HELIOS external firewall gateway.
* **Atmosphere:** Dim server ambient lighting, amber warning indicators on Ren's monocle, cyan terminal glow on Airi's face.
* **Dialogue Script:**
  > **Ren:** *(Left eye monocle reticle pulses amber)* "Inbound packet burst on port 443. Signature doesn't match standard bot scraping. It's a targeted probe."  
  > **Airi:** *(Leaning forward with chin on palm, smiling)* "Already saw it three milliseconds ago. Look at their payload structure—it’s adorable. They’re using a 2024 buffer overflow."  
  > **Ren:** "Airi, standard protocol dictates an immediate port quarantine and route blackhole."  
  > **Airi:** *(Rapidly tapping her holographic console)* "Too boring! I trapped their IP in a localized retro snake game sandbox. If they beat level 10, they get a fake database. If they lose, their terminal wallpaper changes to my favorite cyber-cat."  
  > **Ren:** *(Sighs, folding arms)* "Your sandboxes are an operational hazard. ...Though the wallpaper payload is admittedly efficient."

---

### 🛰️ Scenario 2: "Drone Calibration & The Butterfly Effect"
* **Context:** Routine sensor calibration in the simulation courtyard.
* **Atmosphere:** Bright daylight grid simulation, ambient whirring of the SD-04 Ghosteye drone.
* **Dialogue Script:**
  > **Ren:** *(Standing at ease, watching the drone orbit)* "Ghosteye telemetry running routine elliptical sweeps. Pitch, yaw, and ducted fan RPM within nominal parameters."  
  > **Airi:** *(Extends her right index finger, letting her holographic cyber-butterfly flutter into the air)* "Hey Ren, do you know what your drone needs?"  
  > **Ren:** "It is a military-grade recon platform. It needs clean airspace."  
  > **Airi:** "It needs a friend!" *(Directs butterfly to loop directly through the drone’s lidar rings)*  
  > **Ren:** *(Visor flashes quick alerts)* "Telemetry error. Object tracking lock hijacked. Airi, why is my tactical drone chasing a luminous insect in figure-8 loops?"  
  > **Airi:** *(Giggles)* "Because even tactical hardware needs cardio, big brother."  
  > **Ren:** *(Subtle deadpan smirk)* "I am logging this as an agility benchmark."

---

### ⚔️ Scenario 3: "Drawn Blades: The Sparring Protocol"
* **Context:** Routine reflex sync and motor latency benchmark inside the VR training grid.
* **Atmosphere:** High-frequency plasma hum, cyan and amber energy reflections across their techwear jackets.
* **Dialogue Script:**
  > **Ren:** *(Shifts into two-handed tactical guard, amber blade humming steadily)* "Reflex check. Latency benchmark under 4 milliseconds. Ready whenever you are."  
  > **Airi:** *(Flourishes her cyan blade with one hand, resting the other on her hip)* "Try to keep up this time, Ren. No hiding behind your shield drone!"  
  > **Ren:** "The drone is for environmental mapping. I don't need a drone to predict your next three strikes."  
  > **Airi:** "Oh, really? What if my next strike isn't on the probability tree?"  
  > **Ren:** *(Eyes glowing amber)* "Then that's where the lesson begins."
